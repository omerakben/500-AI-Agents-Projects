# Weekly Review  ·  run Fridays (or when the owner asks)

A roll-up of the week that sets up the next one.

## Steps
1. Read this week's `memory/logs/*.md` (the last 5 entries).
2. Read `memory/decisions.md` for decisions made this week.
3. Produce a review with:
   - **Shipped** — what actually got done.
   - **Slipped** — what didn't, and whether it still matters.
   - **Decisions** — made this week, and any still pending.
   - **Next week's top 3** — the priorities to carry in.
   - **One thing to fix** — a process or pattern worth changing.
4. Save to `reports/weekly-<year>-W<week number>.md` and give the owner the highlights.

> This is what a bundle pack rolls up across all its folders — one review for the
> whole business.
