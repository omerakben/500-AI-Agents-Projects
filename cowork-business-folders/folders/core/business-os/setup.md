# Setup — teach Business OS your business

Do this once. ~20 minutes. You only need step 1 to get your first win.

## 1. Fill in your profile  (required, ~10 min)
Open `memory/profile.md` and complete every section:
- **What we do** — one honest paragraph.
- **Who runs it** — you, and anyone else who makes calls.
- **Key facts** — products/services, main customers, pricing, anything Claude will need often.
- **Hard rules** — the things Claude must never do (e.g. "never quote a price under $X",
  "never contact a client without me").

## 2. Add your people  (~5 min)
Open `memory/people.md` and list the customers, teammates, and vendors that come up a lot.

## 3. Connect your tools  (optional, ~5 min)
Open `connectors.md`. Enable Google Calendar and Gmail if you want meetings and unread
threads pulled into the brief automatically. Skip if you'd rather paste context.

## 4. Get your first win
In Claude Cowork, with this folder open:
> "Give me my morning brief."

You'll get a prioritized brief saved to `reports/`.

## 5. Turn on scheduling
Create two scheduled tasks in Cowork:
- **8:00 weekdays** → the prompt in `schedules/morning-brief.md`
- **18:00 weekdays** → the prompt in `schedules/eod-log.md`

That's it. From here, just talk to the folder — log decisions as they happen, prep for
meetings, and let the brief and log run themselves.
