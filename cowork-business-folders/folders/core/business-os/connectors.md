# Connectors

Enable these in Claude Cowork (Settings → Connectors). All optional — Business OS works
fully with none.

| Connector | Required | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Calendar | No | Pulls today's meetings into the morning brief and powers `meeting-prep`. |
| Gmail | No | Surfaces unread threads that need a decision in the morning brief. |
| Google Drive | No | Lets you reference docs by pointing at them instead of pasting. |

> Start with zero connectors and add them as you feel the friction. The morning brief
> and EOD log work off `memory/` alone; connectors just save you typing.
