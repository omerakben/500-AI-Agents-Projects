# Decision Log

**When to use:** the owner makes (or wants to think through) a decision worth remembering.

## Inputs
- The decision and its context from the owner.
- `memory/decisions.md` — to keep the format and avoid duplicates.

## Steps
1. If the owner is still deciding, lay out the real options with trade-offs — briefly,
   no false balance. Recommend one.
2. Once decided, prepend an entry to `memory/decisions.md` using `templates/decision.md`:
   - Date, the decision in one line.
   - Context (why this came up).
   - Options considered.
   - Decision & rationale.
   - Owner and any review date.
3. If the decision changes a durable fact (pricing, policy, a hard rule), also update
   `memory/profile.md` — with confirmation.

## Output
- A new entry at the top of `memory/decisions.md`.
- Optional profile update.
