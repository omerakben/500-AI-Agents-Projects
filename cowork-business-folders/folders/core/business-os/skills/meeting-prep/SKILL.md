# Meeting Prep

**When to use:** the owner has a meeting coming up, or the morning brief flags one without prep.

## Inputs
- Who the meeting is with and its purpose (from the owner or Calendar).
- `memory/people.md` — history with the attendees.
- `memory/decisions.md` and recent logs — anything relevant that's open.

## Steps
1. Identify the attendees and pull what you know about them from `memory/people.md`.
2. State the meeting's **goal** in one line — the outcome that makes it worth having.
3. Draft a one-page brief:
   - **Goal & desired outcome.**
   - **Context** — where things stand, last interaction.
   - **Talking points** — 3–5, most important first.
   - **Asks / decisions needed** — what you want out of it.
   - **Watch-outs** — sensitivities, open commitments.
4. Save to `reports/meeting-<attendee-or-topic>-<date>.md`.

## Output
- `reports/meeting-<...>.md`, ready to skim right before the call.
