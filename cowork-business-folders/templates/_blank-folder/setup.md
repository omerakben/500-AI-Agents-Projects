# Setup — teach this folder your business

Work through this once. ~20 minutes.

## 1. Fill in your profile
Open `memory/profile.md` and complete every section: what the business does, who
runs it, key facts, and your **hard rules** (things Claude must never do).

## 2. Add your people
Open `memory/people.md` and list the customers, staff, and vendors that come up often.

## 3. Connect your tools
Open `connectors.md` and enable the connectors you want. Skip any you don't use.

## 4. Get your first win
In Claude Cowork, with this folder open, ask:
> "<the same-day-win prompt for this folder>"

## 5. Turn on scheduling
Create a scheduled task in Cowork using the prompt in `schedules/morning-brief.md`.

Done. From now on, just talk to the folder.
